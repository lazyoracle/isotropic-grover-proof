\section{Scope and Trust Boundary of the Machine-Checked Formalization}
\label{sec:supp-overview}

Analytical derivations of high-dimensional spherical integrals and recursive quantum channels routinely suppress measure-theoretic measurability obligations, boundary degeneracies at unit or zero fidelity, and index-parity bookkeeping between real and complex Hilbert spaces. To eliminate any possibility of unstated assumptions across these steps, we formalized all core geometric, Markov-trajectory, quantum-channel, heterogeneous gate-counting, and multi-target Grover theorems of the Main Text in the Lean~4 interactive theorem prover (\texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0})~\cite{MouraUllrich2021,Mathlib2020}, building on recent formalizations of quantum information theory and verified quantum circuits~\cite{Meiburg2025,Shirobokov2026}.

The companion library \texttt{IsotropicGroverProof} comprises $6{,}759$ lines of Lean~4 code organized into eleven verified submodules imported by a single barrel module \texttt{Basic}. Every definition and theorem is checked directly by Lean's small trusted kernel with \texttt{-{}-wfail} enabled so compiler or linter warnings are rejected as build errors. Across the entire repository, there are zero \texttt{sorry} placeholders, zero custom axiom declarations, and zero unverified type-adapter postulates between real sphere measures and complex density operators. Compile-time \texttt{\#print axioms} assertions at the bottom of each module, reinforced by an automated repository-wide continuous-integration (CI) audit rooted at \texttt{IsotropicGroverProof}, verify that every exported theorem depends solely on Lean's three standard foundational axioms: propositional extensionality (\texttt{propext}), classical choice (\texttt{Classical.choice}), and quotient soundness (\texttt{Quot.sound}).

For complete clarity on the verification boundary, \mref{sec:model}{Secs.~II}--\mref{sec:multi-grover}{IV\,A} of the Main Text---spanning the real Euclidean isomorphism $\mathbb{C}^{2^n} \cong \mathbb{R}^{2^{n+1}}$, the closed-interval spherical Poisson kernel on $\sigma \in [0, 1]$, \mref{thm:poisson-moments}{Theorems~1}--\mref{thm:channel-duality}{3}, \mref{cor:multi-grover}{Corollary~1} for $M$-target Grover search, concrete $1$- and $2$-qubit primitive-gate witnesses, and continuity/monotonicity limits---have $1$-to-$1$ machine-checked counterparts in \texttt{IsotropicGroverProof}. Conversely, the continuous first-lobe calculus of \mref{prop:optimal-k}{Proposition~1} (\mref{sec:optimal-stopping}{Sec.~IV\,B}) and the floating-point evaluations of asymptotic query crossover and $60$-qubit resource thresholds (\mref{sec:complexity-resources}{Sec.~V}) are analytical consequences of the verified formula in \mref{cor:multi-grover}{Corollary~1}, audited separately by deterministic unit tests in \texttt{paper/scripts/test\_generate\_data.py}.

\section{Key Formalization Engineering Choices}
\label{sec:supp-engineering}

Direct transliteration of informal physics proofs into dependent type theory often stalls on coordinate singularities, unformalized partial differential equations, or state-dependent measure spaces. Four structural design choices keep the Lean~4 development elementary, coordinate-free, and valid across the entire closed parameter interval $\sigma \in [0, 1]$ and all register sizes $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

\subsection{One-Dimensional Antiderivative Recurrences Instead of Laplace--Beltrami PDEs}
\label{sec:supp-ftc}

Standard textbook proofs of Poisson-kernel moments on $S^{d-1}$ invoke Green's second identity for the Laplace--Beltrami operator on smooth manifolds with boundary~\cite{Axler2001} or orthogonality of Gegenbauer polynomials~\cite{LacallePozoCoronado2019}. Formalizing multivariable Stokes theorems on curved $(d - 1)$-spheres in \texttt{Mathlib} would require substantial differential-geometric machinery unrelated to quantum circuits. Instead, \texttt{IsotropicError} defines the polar-angle law directly on $\mathbb{R}$ across the closed interval $\sigma \in [0, 1]$ via \texttt{poissonAngleMeasure}:
\begin{equation}
\nu_{d,\sigma} =
\begin{cases}
Z_d(\sigma)^{-1} g_d(\,\cdot\,; \sigma)\,\lambda\rvert_{[0,\pi]}, & \sigma < 1, \\[3pt]
\delta_0, & \sigma \ge 1
\end{cases},
\label{eq:supp-angle-measure}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda\rvert_{[0,\pi]}$ is Lebesgue measure restricted to $[0, \pi]$ weighted via \texttt{volume.withDensity} by \texttt{poissonKernelDensity}, $Z_d(\sigma)$ is \texttt{poissonNormConst}, and $\delta_0$ is \texttt{Measure.dirac} at $0$. In the decoherent limit $\sigma = 0$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:supp-angle-measure} reduces directly to the polar-angle marginal $\propto \sin^{d-2}\theta\,d\theta$ of uniform Haar measure on $S^{d-1}$, whereas at $\sigma = 1$ the point mass $\delta_0$ avoids the $0/0$ singularity in $(1 - \sigma^2)/D(\sigma, 0)^{d/2}$ without case-splitting downstream theorems. Differentiating the three bounded $\mathrm{C}^1$ products of \mref{eq:three-antiderivs}{Eq.~(8)} in the Main Text and applying \texttt{intervalIntegral}'s Fundamental Theorem of Calculus establishes \texttt{poissonAngleMeasure\_mean\_cos} and \texttt{poissonAngleMeasure\_cos\_sq} for all $d \ge 2$ by two-step induction $d \mapsto d + 2$ from $d = 2$ and $d = 3$. In parallel, \texttt{Gegenbauer} formalizes the algebraic Laplacian cancellation $\Delta(\ip{\mathbf{x}}{\Psi}^2 - \norm{\mathbf{x}}^2/d) = 0$ (\texttt{laplacian\_trace\_cancellation}, \texttt{harmonicExtension\_eval}), explicitly linking the calculus proof to harmonic polynomial theory.

\subsection{Coordinate-Free Ambient Reflections on the Orthogonal Equator}
\label{sec:supp-reflections}

In Monte Carlo statevector simulations~\cite{SahaRoyLacalle2026}, sampling $\mathbf{e}_2 \sim \mu_\perp(\Psi)$ requires Gram--Schmidt orthogonalization of $d - 1 = 2^{n+1} - 1$ tangent vectors and nested spherical coordinates $(\theta_1, \dots, \theta_{d-2})$. Formalizing iterated trigonometric Jacobians in arbitrary dimension is brittle and unnecessary. In \texttt{CrossTerm}, we define the orthogonal complement \texttt{V\_perp} as a closed real \texttt{Submodule} of \texttt{E} $n$ of rank $d - 1 \ge 1$ (\texttt{V\_perp\_finrank\_pos}), construct the normalized rotation-invariant sphere measure on \texttt{Metric.sphere} inside \texttt{V\_perp} via \texttt{Measure.haar}, and push it forward into the ambient space \texttt{E} $n$ to obtain \texttt{perpSphereMeasure}.

{\setlength{\emergencystretch}{4.5em}%
Any ambient linear isometry $L : \texttt{E}\ n \simeq_{\ell i}[\mathbb{R}] \texttt{E}\ n$ fixing $\Psi$ stabilizes \texttt{V\_perp} and preserves the induced Haar measure \texttt{perpSphereMeasure} (\texttt{perpSphereMeasure\_map\_isometry}). Applying central inversion $-\mathrm{id}$ kills all odd linear moments (\texttt{integral\_perp\_inner\_eq\_zero}), while sign reflections across $(\mathbb{R}\mathbf{b}_i)^\perp$ and Householder swaps across $(\mathbb{R}(\mathbf{b}_i - \mathbf{b}_j))^\perp$ diagonalize and equalize the second-moment quadratic form in \texttt{SecondMoment}. Combining \texttt{perpSphere\_inner\_mul\_inner} with \texttt{poissonAngleMeasure\_cos\_sq} proves \texttt{isotropicError\_bilinear\_secondMoment} for all unit $\Psi \in S^{d-1}$, $\sigma \in [0, 1]$, and $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \texttt{E}\ n$:
\begin{equation}
\int_0^\pi \int_{S(V_\perp(\Psi))} \ip{\Psi'}{\mathbf{u}}\ip{\Psi'}{\mathbf{v}}\,d\mu_\perp(\Psi)(\mathbf{e}_2)\,d\nu_{d,\sigma}(\theta) = \sigma^2 \ip{\Psi}{\mathbf{u}}\ip{\Psi}{\mathbf{v}} + \frac{1 - \sigma^2}{2^{n+1}}\ip{\mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{v}},
\label{eq:supp-bilinear}
\end{equation}
where $\Psi' = (\cos\theta)\Psi + (\sin\theta)\mathbf{e}_2$ is \texttt{isotropicError} and $d = 2^{n+1}$. At $\sigma = 1$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:supp-bilinear} reduces to the noiseless product $\ip{\Psi}{\mathbf{u}}\ip{\Psi}{\mathbf{v}}$, i.e., zero perturbation; at $\sigma = 0$, it reduces to the isotropic Euclidean inner product $\ip{\mathbf{u}}{\mathbf{v}} / 2^{n+1}$ with all $(2^{n+1} - 1)$ tangent factors canceled.\par}

\subsection{Local Householder Charts and Parameter Continuity of Markov Trajectories}
\label{sec:supp-householder}

A subtle measurability issue arises in multi-gate trajectories (\texttt{Sequential}): in Eq.~\eqref{eq:supp-bilinear}, the inner integration domain $S(V_\perp(\Psi))$ varies with the random state $\Psi \in S^{d-1}$ produced by earlier noisy gates. To recurse backward over a heterogeneous gate schedule \texttt{steps : List ((E n $\simeq_{\ell i}$[$\mathbb{R}$] E n) $\times$ $\mathbb{R}$)} without Fubini product-space measurable-selection theorems over moving submanifolds, \texttt{Sequential} defines \texttt{noisyTrajectoryExpectationHet} by structural recursion on the gate list and proves that each conditional expectation operator preserves continuity on \texttt{unitSphere} (\texttt{noisyTrajectoryExpectationHet\_continuousOn\_unitSphere}).

Specifically, to prove continuity near any reference state $\Psi_0 \in S^{d-1}$, we take $\Psi \in S^{d-1}$ in the open hemisphere $\ip{\Psi_0}{\Psi} > -1$ (so $\Psi_0 + \Psi \ne 0$) and transport $\mathbf{e}_2 \in S(V_\perp(\Psi_0))$ onto $S(V_\perp(\Psi))$ via the smooth Householder reflection across $(\mathbb{R}(\Psi_0 + \Psi))^\perp$:
\begin{equation}
R_{\Psi_0, \Psi}(\mathbf{z}) = \frac{2\ip{\Psi_0 + \Psi}{\mathbf{z}}}{\norm{\Psi_0 + \Psi}^2}(\Psi_0 + \Psi) - \mathbf{z},
\label{eq:supp-householder}
\end{equation}
where $\norm{\Psi_0} = \norm{\Psi} = 1$ implies $R_{\Psi_0, \Psi}(\Psi_0) = \Psi$ and hence $R_{\Psi_0, \Psi}(V_\perp(\Psi_0)) = V_\perp(\Psi)$. When $\Psi \to \Psi_0$, $\Psi_0 + \Psi \to 2\Psi_0$ gives $R_{\Psi_0, \Psi_0}(\mathbf{e}_2) = -\mathbf{e}_2$ on $V_\perp(\Psi_0)$, while isometric invariance of Haar measure pulls the integral over $\mu_\perp(\Psi)$ back to the fixed measure $\mu_\perp(\Psi_0)$. Extending continuous observables radially from $S^{d-1}$ to bounded continuous functions on \texttt{E} $n$ and applying dominated convergence across $(\theta, \mathbf{e}_2) \in [0, \pi] \times S(V_\perp(\Psi_0))$ establishes sphere continuity and finite-sum linearity (\texttt{noisyTrajectoryExpectationHet\_add\_of\_continuousOn}, \texttt{noisyTrajectoryExpectationHet\_sum}), from which \texttt{noisyTrajectoryHet\_bilinear} and \texttt{noisyTrajectoryHet\_succProb} follow cleanly by list induction.

\subsection{Syntactic Circuit Induction and Heterogeneous Gate-Count Factorization}
\label{sec:supp-circuit-induction}

{\setlength{\emergencystretch}{5em}%
To connect real-sphere trajectories to standard quantum-information channels and circuit compilers, \texttt{QuantumChannel} constructs explicit norm-preserving bijections \texttt{vecToReal} and \texttt{realToVec} ($\mathbb{C}^{2^n} \leftrightarrow \texttt{E}\ n$), lifts unitaries $U \in \mathbf{U}[\mathrm{Fin}(2^n)]$ to real linear isometries via \texttt{unitaryToRealIso}, and proves five-way Born-rule equivalence (\texttt{succProb\_ketToReal\_eq\_born}) between real rank-2 coordinate norms, squared ket amplitudes, pure-state matrix diagonals, projector expectation values, and \texttt{computationalPOVM} outcome probabilities. Averaging \texttt{pureDensityMatrix} entrywise via \texttt{noisyTrajectoryExpectation$\mathbb{C}$Het} pairs matching-parity basis vectors on real parts while canceling opposite-parity inner products on imaginary parts (\texttt{noisyTrajectoryHet\_pureDensityMatrix\_re}, \texttt{noisyTrajectoryHet\_pureDensityMatrix\_im}), proving exact agreement with \texttt{depolarizingChannel} in \texttt{noisyTrajectoryHet\_eq\_depolarizingChannel}.\par}

At the syntactic circuit layer, an $n$-qubit program \texttt{c : Circuit n} in \emph{formal-quantum-computation} is built inductively from \texttt{Circuit.identity}, primitive gate applications \texttt{Circuit.gate} $g$ $r$, and sequential composition \texttt{Circuit.seq} $c_1$ $c_2$. Given any per-gate fidelity assignment $\sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}} : \forall \{k\},\, \texttt{PrimitiveGate}\ k \to \mathbb{R}$ taking values in $[0, 1]$, \texttt{noisyCircuitChannelHet} attaches $\Lambda_{\sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}(g)}$ after each primitive gate while leaving \texttt{Circuit.identity} noiseless. Using unitary commutation (\texttt{depolarizingChannel\_comm\_ofUnitary}) and multiplicative composition (\texttt{depolarizingChannel\_compose}), structural induction on $c$ proves \texttt{noisyCircuitChannelHet\_eq}, and case-splitting $g$ over all nine constructors of \texttt{PrimitiveGate} proves \texttt{circuitFidelity\_eq\_prod\_count}:
\begin{align}
\operatorname{circuitFidelity}(\sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}, c) &= \Biggl(\prod_{g_1 \in \{X,Y,Z,H,S,T\}} \sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}(g_1)^{\mathrm{count}(g_1, c)}\Biggr) \nonumber \\
&\quad \times \sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}(\mathrm{CNOT})^{\mathrm{count}(\mathrm{CNOT}, c)} \sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}(\mathrm{SWAP})^{\mathrm{count}(\mathrm{SWAP}, c)} \sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}(\mathrm{TOFF})^{\mathrm{count}(\mathrm{TOFF}, c)},
\label{eq:supp-prod-count}
\end{align}
where $\mathrm{count}(g, c)$ denotes \texttt{Circuit.count}. When $\sigma_{\mathrm{Gate}}$ depends only on gate arity ($\sigma_1$, $\sigma_2$, and $\sigma_3$ on $1$-, $2$-, and $3$-qubit gates), \texttt{circuitFidelity\_arity} collapses Eq.~\eqref{eq:supp-prod-count} to $\sigma_1^{\texttt{gateCount1Q}(c)} \sigma_2^{\texttt{gateCount2Q}(c)} \sigma_3^{\texttt{gateCount3Q}(c)}$ with $\texttt{gateCount1Q}(c) + \texttt{gateCount2Q}(c) + \texttt{gateCount3Q}(c) = \texttt{c.size}$ (\texttt{gateCount\_sum\_eq\_size}), recovering uniform $\sigma^{\texttt{c.size}}$ when $\sigma_1 = \sigma_2 = \sigma_3 = \sigma$.

\section{Reproducible Verification Guide}
\label{sec:supp-reproducibility}

All Lean~4 proofs, figure datasets, manuscript style checks, and PDF build targets can be reproduced deterministically from the repository root. With the Lean version manager \texttt{elan} installed, fetching the precompiled \texttt{Mathlib} cache and compiling the entire \texttt{IsotropicGroverProof} library with warnings treated as errors requires a single command:
\begin{verbatim}
lake exe cache get && lake build --wfail
\end{verbatim}
To inspect the foundational axiom footprint of the primary theorems interactively through the Lean kernel without relying on pre-recorded build artifacts, run:
\begin{verbatim}
lake env lean --stdin <<'EOF'
import IsotropicGroverProof
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.poissonAngleMeasure_cos_sq
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.isotropicError_bilinear_secondMoment
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyTrajectoryHet_bilinear
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyCircuitChannelHet_eq
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyCircuitHet_trajectory_eq_channel
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.circuitFidelity_eq_prod_count
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.circuitFidelity_arity
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyCircuitHet_subsetBornProb
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyMultiGroverHet_succProb
#print axioms IsotropicGrover.noisyGrover_circuit_grover2_bornProb
EOF
\end{verbatim}
Every command above prints strictly \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}. Finally, to verify that all numerical figure/table files in \texttt{paper/data/} match \texttt{paper/scripts/generate\_data.py}, run the style and Lean-identifier linter across all manuscript sources, compile \texttt{main.pdf}, \texttt{supplement.pdf}, and \texttt{arxiv.pdf} via \texttt{latexmk}, and audit all three TeX logs for undefined references, duplicate hyperlink anchors, or overfull boxes, execute:
\begin{verbatim}
bash scripts/build_paper.sh
\end{verbatim}
Both the Lean~4 kernel build/axiom-audit workflow and \texttt{bash scripts/build\_paper.sh} run automatically on every push and pull request in GitHub Actions CI (\texttt{.github/workflows/lean\_action\_ci.yml}).
